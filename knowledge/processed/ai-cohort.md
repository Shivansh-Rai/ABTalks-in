# AI Cohort

The AI Cohort is a 31-day program for building and deploying a production-grade enterprise AI chatbot.

Core facts:
- 31 days end to end
- 8 gated phases
- Roughly 2–4 hours per day
- Free for participants
- Uses free tools such as Ollama, Groq, and Chroma

Coverage includes RAG, embeddings, agents, MCP, guardrails, Docker, Kubernetes, evaluation, and deployment.
