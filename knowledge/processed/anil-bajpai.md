# Anil Bajpai

People also ask this as: who runs Klymb AI · who runs abtalks · who founded
ABTalks · who is the CEO · who owns this platform.

Anil Bajpai is the founder and CEO behind Klymb AI (formerly ABTalks).

He leads Klymb AI, an AI-first learning and talent ecosystem focused
on helping students and early-career professionals build practical
skills, create visible proof of work, and become more discoverable
to employers.

Through Klymb AI, he drives initiatives including build-in-public
challenges, AI learning programs, workshops, hackathons, and
career-focused community activities.

## Role at Klymb AI

- Founder and CEO
- Leads the Klymb AI learning and talent ecosystem
- Drives the community's focus on practical building and proof of work
- Hosts and participates in Klymb AI conversations around AI, careers,
  technology, leadership, and the future of work
