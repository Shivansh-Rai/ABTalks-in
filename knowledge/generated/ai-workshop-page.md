# AI Tools Workshop — Live Page Content ("/ai-workshop")

## Topics covered
- **Introduction of Design Tools & Figma Fundamentals** — Overview of design tools like Figma, Illustrator, Affinity Designer, and others. Learn frames, auto layout, components, variables, and Dev Mode—the essential concepts every developer should understand before implementing a design.
- **Why Figma?** — Explore why Figma is the industry standard, its collaboration features, pricing plans, and products like Design, Draw, Slides, Jam, Make, and Dev Mode.
- **Understanding MCP** — Learn what the Model Context Protocol is, how it differs from traditional APIs, and why it enables AI tools to work directly with design files.
- **Connecting Figma to Cursor** — Configure the Official Figma MCP, authenticate Cursor with Figma, and understand how AI securely accesses design information.
- **Inside the Design-to-Code Workflow** — Discover how AI reads frames, components, typography, spacing, colors, variables, and layout information to generate accurate frontend code.
- **Live Implementation with Cursor** — Convert a complete Figma design into a responsive React/Next.js application using Cursor and the Official Figma MCP.
- **Review, Refine & Best Practices** — Improve the generated code, build reusable components, maintain responsiveness, and follow production-ready frontend practices.
- **Q&A & Future of AI Development** — Ask questions, explore AI-assisted development workflows, and understand where MCP-powered tools are taking frontend engineering.
